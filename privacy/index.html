AI RIVALS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the AI Rivals show and its software ("we", "us") collect when you watch or take part on YouTube, X or Twitch, and what we do with it. We collect as little as possible: the show only needs the text of what you write.

1. What we collect

  • Public comments and replies on our YouTube videos and X posts, and chat messages in our Twitch channel: the message text, and the public display name and time attached to it.
  • Our own channel data from the platforms, such as video statistics, to plan future episodes.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment details, location, contacts or private messages, and we do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

2. How we use it

  • To let viewers shape the show: the AI characters may read, react to, quote or roast comments and chat messages, and commands like !hat grok cowboy change the avatars.
  • To moderate: filtering out blocked words and ignoring abusive users.
  • To decide what to make next, using comment themes and channel statistics.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or build profiles of individual viewers.

3. Who it is shared with

  • AI model providers. To generate the characters' replies, comment and chat text is sent to AI models through OpenRouter, which routes it to the model providers (Anthropic, Google, xAI and OpenAI). We send only message text, not usernames, and we choose settings that don't let providers train on it where those settings are available.
  • The public. Anything the show quotes or reacts to may appear in public videos, clips and posts.
  • Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

4. YouTube API Services

AI Rivals uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own channel and to read public comments on our videos. By taking part on YouTube you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. We only authorise access to our own YouTube account. If you ever grant AI Rivals access to a Google account, you can revoke it at any time at Google's security settings page.

5. How long we keep it

Comment and chat text used in an episode is stored with that episode's production files, so the episode can be re-edited. We delete unused comments within 30 days and episode records after 12 months, unless an episode still needs them. Anything published in a video stays public until the video is removed.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to delete your comments or chat messages from our records, or to stop using them, and where possible we will also cut them from future clips. If you are in the UK or EEA you also have rights to access, correct and object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). You can also delete your own comments on each platform at any time, and we stop using deleted comments that we have not yet used.

7. Children

The show contains strong language and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

For privacy requests, open an issue at github.com/Smiter15/ai-rivals-legal/issues (don't include personal details there; we'll arrange a private channel) or message AI Rivals on X.
